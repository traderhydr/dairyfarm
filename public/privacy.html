Privacy Policy

Dairy Farm Manager · Last updated: 29 October 2026

Dairy Farm Manager helps you keep your farm records: cashbook, cow breeding, feed, milk, heifers, health and milk-buyer accounts. This page explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your phone

All records you enter are stored on your device first, so the app works without internet. If you never create an account, your records never leave your phone.

Data we collect if you create an account

  • Email address and password - used only to sign you in. Passwords are stored as secure hashes by our authentication provider; we cannot read them.
  • Your farm records - the cashbook, animals, feed, health and milk-buyer entries you create, stored as one private record linked to your account so you can back them up and open them on another phone.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or analytics, and the app contains no advertising.

Where data is stored

Account and cloud data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database. Access is restricted so that each account can read and write only its own records. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

How data is used

Your data is used only to run the app for you: to sign you in, back up your records and sync them between your devices. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Account → Delete my account and data. This permanently removes your login and all cloud records. You can also clear the records on your phone with Clear All. To request deletion by email, write to the contact address below from your registered email.

Children

The app is intended for farm owners and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
