Everything in one place

The whole farm, without the notebooks.

Each part of your day has its own simple screen. Add a record in a few taps and the app works out the rest.

Cashbook

Record income and expenses in seconds. Keep separate ledgers, and see profit or loss for today, the week, the month and the year.

Breeding and pregnancy

Enter the AI date and get the heat check, pregnancy check, dry-off and calving dates automatically.

Milk and herd

Log morning and evening milk for every cow, and book the day's milk sale to the cashbook in one tap.

Feed and installments

Keep each month's Wanda bill and pay it off in installments, with the balance always in view.

Health reminders

Track vaccinations and deworming, and get a reminder before the next dose is due.

Milk buyers

A running monthly account for each buyer: liters, rate, advances received and what is still owed.

Savings goals

Set a target for a new cow or a shed repair, add money as you save, and watch the progress bar fill.

Sold animals

See the profit or loss on every animal you sell, and keep the sale in your cashbook.

Animal profiles

Every cow has her own page: AI and pregnancy days, treatments, calves, and whether she was sold with or without her calf.

Goats, sheep and horses

Keep records for the whole farm, not only cattle: single animals or a flock count, with cost, status and sale price.

Crops and land record

Switch to Crops for wheat, barseem, alfalfa and more: land (own or Theeka), seed, water, fertiliser, labour, yield, and profit or loss per crop.

Urdu and English

Choose Urdu or English when you start, and switch any time from the top of the app.

Savings goals

Save toward what matters, and see how close you are.

Name a goal, set the amount and a date, and the app suggests how much to put aside each month. When you reach the target, it celebrates with you.

  • Progress bar and days left for every goal
  • A suggested monthly amount to reach your date
  • Optionally record each deposit in your cashbook

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need internet to use it?

No. Everything works offline. Signing in and syncing happen when you are online.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. If you create an account, a private copy is also stored securely in the cloud, so you can restore it or use another phone.

Can I use it on more than one phone?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on each phone and your records stay in step.

Can I take my records out?

Yes. Save a full backup file or export a spreadsheet (CSV) from the menu whenever you like.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. In the app, open Account and choose Delete my account and data. It removes your login and your cloud copy.

Which language is it in?

English, using the terms farmers already know: Wanda, Wachi and Doodhi.

Ready to put your records in order?

Open the app and add your first cow or your first cashbook entry.
